Theory

A circular aperture of diameter D diffracts light into an Airy disc. Two point objects are just resolved (Rayleigh criterion) when the central maximum of one falls on the first minimum of the other:

θ = 1.22 · λ / D   (radians)

Smaller wavelength or larger aperture improves resolution (smaller θ). Resolving power of a telescope increases with D/λ. Convert radians to arcseconds with × 206265.

Precautions & Standard Errors

  • Use consistent SI units: convert λ (nm) to metres and D (mm) to metres before dividing.
  • Keep the aperture clean and circular; obstruction raises the effective limit.
  • Record λ from a calibrated source; colour filters drift with age.
