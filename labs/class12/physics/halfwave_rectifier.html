Theory

A half-wave rectifier passes only the positive half-cycle of the transformer secondary. With peak input Vp (ideal diode):

Vdc = Vp / π   ≈ 0.318 · Vp

With a shunt capacitor filter C across load R (mains f = 50 Hz, Idc = Vdc / R), the approximate ripple is:

Vr ≈ Idc / (f · C)

Larger R or C lowers ripple. Ripple factor r = Vr(rms) / Vdc; without a filter r ≈ 1.21 for half-wave.

Precautions & Standard Errors

  • Observe diode polarity; reverse connection gives no output.
  • Respect capacitor polarity and voltage rating; discharge before handling.
  • Use a regulated secondary and measure Vp with CRO on the same ground reference.
