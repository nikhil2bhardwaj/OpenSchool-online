Theory

A meter bridge is a practical form of the Wheatstone bridge. At the null point the bridge is balanced and

X / R = (100 − L) / L  ⟹  X = R · (100 − L) / L

The unknown wire resistance X, its length ℓ and measured diameter d give the resistivity:

ρ = X · A / ℓ = X · π(d/2)² / ℓ

Precautions & Standard Errors

  • All connections must be tight and the wire uniform; clean the ends with sandpaper.
  • Make the null point fall near the middle of the wire (30–70 cm) for best accuracy.
  • Do not press the jockey hard; measure length from the same end each time.
  • Account for end resistances at both ends (left +0.05 cm, right +0.08 cm).
  • Insert the key only momentarily while testing for the null to avoid heating.

End corrections applied

Left end +0.05 cm · Right end +0.08 cm are folded into the simulated bridge.
