Theory

Determine the focal length of a convex mirror using a convex auxiliary lens.

\frac{1}{f}=\frac{1}{v}+\frac{1}{u}

A convex mirror always forms a virtual, diminished image, so its focal length is found indirectly with an auxiliary convex lens that produces a real image, and the mirror formula is then applied.

Precautions & Standard Errors

  • Read all scales with the eye perpendicular to the scale to avoid parallax error.
  • Check the apparatus for zero error before starting and apply a correction if present.
  • Take at least five readings spread across the range and use their mean.
  • Keep the apparatus clean, dry and free from contamination between observations.
  • Note the least count of every instrument and record readings to the correct number of decimals.

Troubleshooting

  • F_NOT_AXIAL: Fuzzy or doubled image — Object, lens and image not on one principal axis. Remedy: Adjust all uprights to the same height and axis.
  • F_INDEX_ERROR: Object distances systematically wrong — Upright index not aligned with the scale zero. Remedy: Determine and apply the index correction.
