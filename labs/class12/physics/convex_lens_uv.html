Theory

For a thin convex lens the lens formula (magnitudes for a real image) is

1/f = 1/v − 1/u  ⟹  f = u·v / (u + v)

Plotting u against v gives a rectangular hyperbola; plotting 1/u against 1/v gives a straight line of slope −1 whose intercepts give 1/f. The focal length is read from the intercepts rather than from a single pair.

Precautions & Standard Errors

  • The object, lens and image needle tips must all lie on the same principal axis.
  • Determine the index correction for each upright before recording positions.
  • Remove parallax by moving the eye sideways until the image and needle show no relative movement.
  • Keep the object distance greater than the focal length to obtain a real image.
  • Choose a distant object to obtain an approximate value of f before precise u–v readings.
