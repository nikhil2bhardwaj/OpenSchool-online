Theory

Root-tip cells of onion undergo mitosis. Prophase: chromatin condenses; Metaphase: chromosomes align on equatorial plate; Anaphase: sister chromatids separate; Telophase: two nuclei re-form. Identification score rises with magnification and stage clarity.

Identifiability = base(stage) + k · log(mag)    (deterministic model)

Precautions & Standard Errors

  • Use only the growing root tip (2–3 mm) and hydrolyse for the fixed time.
  • Stain adequately with acetocarmine; squash gently to spread cells in one layer.
  • Observe under low power first, then high power; avoid air bubbles.
