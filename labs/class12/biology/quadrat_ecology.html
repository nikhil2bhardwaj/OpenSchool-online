Theory

Population density is the number of individuals per unit area, estimated by counting plants inside randomly placed quadrats and averaging over replicates.

Density = Count / Quadrat area (individuals m⁻²) — deterministic

Precautions & Standard Errors

  • Place quadrats randomly; do not choose dense patches deliberately.
  • Count edge individuals by a fixed rule (e.g. only top + left edges).
  • Take several replicate quadrats and report the mean density.

Triage

Double-counting edge plants (fault) inflates density systematically by 20%.
