Theory

Germinating seeds respire aerobically, releasing CO2 which turns lime water milky (calcium carbonate formation). Respiration rate rises with living mass and with temperature (Q10 ≈ 2) up to an optimum, then falls as enzymes denature.

CO2 % = 0.02 · M · 1.08^(T−20) · tempFactor   |   turbidity = min(100, CO2·40)

M = seed mass (g), T = °C. tempFactor = 1 below 35 °C, declining to 0.4 at 45 °C.

Precautions & Standard Errors

  • Use actively germinating (not dry, not boiled) seeds of uniform size.
  • Keep the delivery tube airtight and the lime water freshly prepared and clear.
  • Maintain constant temperature in a water bath during each observation.
  • Dead seeds (fault) release almost no CO2 — the control with boiled seeds must stay clear.
