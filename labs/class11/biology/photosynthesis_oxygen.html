Theory

Hydrilla photosynthesis releases O2, seen as bubbles that collect in an inverted test tube over a funnel. The bubble rate saturates with both light and CO2 (limiting-factor principle): raising either factor helps only while it is limiting.

bubbles/min = 60 · L/(L+25) · C/(C+0.20)

L = light %, C = dissolved CO2 %. Test that the gas rekindles a glowing splinter (O2 test).

Precautions & Standard Errors

  • Use a healthy Hydrilla twig with a fresh cut end facing upward into the funnel stem.
  • Keep the water CO2-enriched equally (pinch of baking soda) except when it is the variable.
  • Acclimatise the setup at each light distance before counting for a full minute.
  • Overheated water (fault) holds less CO2 and damages enzymes — bubble rate collapses.
