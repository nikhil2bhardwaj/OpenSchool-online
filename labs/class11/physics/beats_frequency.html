Theory

Two waves of nearly equal frequency interfere to produce waxing and waning loudness. The beat frequency equals the magnitude of the difference of the two frequencies.

f_beat = | f₁ − f₂ |    y = sin(2πf₁t) + sin(2πf₂t)

Precautions & Standard Errors

  • Sound the forks with equal, gentle strokes so amplitudes stay comparable.
  • Count beats over at least 10 s to reduce stopwatch reaction error.
  • Work in a quiet room; background noise masks minima.
