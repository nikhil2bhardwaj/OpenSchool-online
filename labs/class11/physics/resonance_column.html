Theory

A closed air column resonates when L = (2n−1)v/4f. At the first resonance L₁ = v/4f, so v = 4fL₁. With two positions, end correction cancels: v = 2f(L₂−L₁).

v = 4·f·L₁    and    v = 2·f·(L₂ − L₁)

Precautions & Standard Errors

  • Raise/lower the reservoir slowly so the water meniscus reading is steady.
  • Apply end correction e = 0.6r when high accuracy is needed.
