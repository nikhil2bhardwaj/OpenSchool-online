Theory

A wire of length L, tension T and mass per unit length μ vibrates in its fundamental mode with f = (1/2L)·√(T/μ). Frequency rises with tension and falls with length; plotting f against 1/L gives a straight line.

f = (1 / 2L) · √(T / μ)    μ = 0.001 kg/m

Precautions & Standard Errors

  • Keep the tuning fork struck gently and the wire free of kinks.
  • Measure vibrating length between the bridge edges without parallax.
  • Keep tension constant while varying length; lubricate the pulley.
