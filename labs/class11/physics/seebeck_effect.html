Theory

Junctions of two dissimilar metals held at different temperatures generate a thermoelectric EMF proportional to the temperature difference: V = S·ΔT, where S is the Seebeck coefficient of the pair.

V = S · ΔT    S(Cu–Fe) = 12 µV/°C, S(Cu–Constantan) = 40 µV/°C

Precautions & Standard Errors

  • Keep the cold junction in melting ice (0 °C) throughout the run.
  • Use a sensitive millivoltmeter and avoid stray thermal EMFs in leads.
  • Stir the hot bath so the junction temperature equals the thermometer reading.
