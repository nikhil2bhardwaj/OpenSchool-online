Theory

Germination needs water, suitable temperature and air. Too little water prevents imbibition; excess water causes waterlogging. Germination peaks near 10 mL water and 25 °C, falling off on either side — modelled deterministically below.

G(w,T) = 100 · max(0, 1 − ((w−10)/9)²) · e^(−(T−25)²/72)

Precautions & Standard Errors

  • Use equal-sized healthy seeds; equal seeds per dish.
  • Add measured water with a graduated pipette.
  • Keep dishes at the same light and temperature.
  • Count germination (radicle ≥ 2 mm) at a fixed time.
