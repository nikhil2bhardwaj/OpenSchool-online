Theory

Osmosis is diffusion of water across a semipermeable membrane from low solute to high solute concentration. The osmotic pressure driving water entry follows van't Hoff's law. The rise of liquid in the osmometer tube (water gain) is proportional to π.

π = iCRT   ·   gain = k · π,   i = 1 (sucrose), R = 0.0831 L·bar·mol⁻¹·K⁻¹, k = 1.8 mL/bar

Higher external solute concentration and higher absolute temperature both raise π and hence the measured gain.

Precautions & Standard Errors

  • Ensure the membrane is intact and tied without leaks.
  • Mark the initial level accurately; avoid parallax.
  • Keep temperature constant during one set of readings.
  • Use freshly prepared sugar solutions of exact molarity.
